// SPECS

What you're walking through

This is a real 1-bedroom rental in Budgewoi, NSW. ~9.7 × 3.7 × 12.6 metres. Captured 1 June 2026 with iPhone 14 Pro LiDAR. ~83,000 vertices. LiDAR mesh + photogrammetry texture. This is the actual deliverable spec an agent or owner gets.

// OPEN IT

Three ways to view

Desktop: drag, scroll, WASD
Phone: tap, swipe, hit Gyro for head-tracking
VR: hit "Enter VR" on Quest 3, Vision Pro, or any WebXR-capable headset. The browser opens the tour in immersive mode.

// HOTSPOTS

Click the orange dots

Hotspots are placed at key objects in the scene. Click them to reveal a card with notes. In a real estate deliverable, hotspots can hold photos, room dimensions, fixtures, and call-to-action links to book an inspection.
