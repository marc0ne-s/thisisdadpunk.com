// WHO

THE PUNK SURVEYOR

One person. iPhone Pro, Osmo Pocket 3, Mac mini. Budgewoi, NSW. Central Coast, Sydney, Hunter. Local-first, no cloud nonsense, punk-textured.

Why I'm doing this

Two reasons. First: the real estate industry is about to have a Matterport moment, and **the whole market is going to look the same**. Domain/CoStar is rolling out 3D tours nationally from 1 July 2026, every agent will get a tour as table stakes, and the look — clean, corporate, sterile — will be identical. Differentiation dies.

Second: there's a real gap for **the spaces that don't get Matterport**. Rentals under $500/wk. Creative venues. Skate parks. The independent Airbnb. The studios, garages, and weird places where a corporate scan doesn't fit and a real human with a phone and a Mac does.

Punk Surveyor is the answer to both. Same quality output (Gaussian splat, WebXR), different aesthetic (raw, textured, lived-in), different pricing (no monthly), different audience (the people Matterport ignores).

// PRINCIPLES

FOUR THINGS I WON'T DO

No cloud lock-in

Your data is yours. I host the tour on a simple URL, hand you the raw files, and you can move them anywhere at any time. If my host goes down, your data is still on your drive.

No monthly subscription

You pay once for the capture. Hosting is included for the first year. After that, A$20/year if you want to keep it live, or you can self-host the files yourself (I give you everything).

No sterile corporate scan

Other services give you a clean, generic 3D model. We capture the room — the scuff marks, the light leaks, the lived-in feel. The aesthetic is punk, not corporate. Buyers and tenants see what they'll actually walk into.

No fake before-it's-ready

If a feature doesn't exist yet, I don't pretend it does on the website. The viewer you see on the proof page is the actual deliverable, not a mock. The price is quoted per job, not invented upfront.
