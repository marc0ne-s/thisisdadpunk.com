// SUB-CONTRACTING

CAN I WORK FOR MATTERPORT?

Yes. And other capture networks. Honest take on how it works and whether it's worth it.

The offer

Matterport has a Capture Services Program. You get routed jobs in your area, you shoot with their gear or your LiDAR-equipped iPhone, you upload to their cloud, they bill the client and pay you a per-scan fee. Same deal exists (in various forms) with Splat Labs, iGUIDE, Giraffe360's service-provider network, and a bunch of regional agencies.

The catch

The per-scan fee is real but modest — typically A$80-150/scan for the technician. You rent yourself to a brand that competes with your own direct clients. The data is often locked in their cloud. You give up your brand at the moment of conversion.

When it makes sense

→ Volume play to fill slow weeks
→ Building a track record before going direct
→ Regional areas where direct clients are sparse
→ You genuinely don't want to run a marketing/sales side

When it doesn't

→ You can find your own clients (you can)
→ You want the brand & the story & the data
→ You're already established (you are, in your own work)
→ You want the highest margin per scan

My recommendation

Do a mix. Direct (highest margin, your brand) as the primary. White-label subcontracting (steady volume, no client work) as the secondary. Skip the Matterport capture program unless volume is the only thing you need. Use Splat Labs or a similar partner if you want splat hosting without the gsplat-on-Mac headache.
