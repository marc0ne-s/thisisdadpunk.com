// 02 — WHY

OTHER SCANS LOOK LIKE VIDEO GAMES. YOURS LOOKS LIKE THE ROOM.

A

Photoreal, not sanitised

Gaussian splat rendering captures the actual light, texture, and lived-in feel of the space. Not a clean, generic 3D model. The scuffs. The light leaks. The texture of the carpet. Buyers and tenants see what they'll actually walk into.

B

WebXR first, not app-first

Open the link in a Quest 3, Vision Pro, or phone. Walk through the space in VR. No app to install. No proprietary hardware. The same link works on desktop for your buyers who don't have headsets.

C

One-off, not subscription

You pay once for the capture. You own the data. Hosting is free or one-shot. No $30-100/month to keep your listing live. No per-scan fee to a SaaS that holds your work hostage.
