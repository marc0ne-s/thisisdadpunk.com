THE PROCESS

From arrival to deliverable in 48 hours. No proprietary hardware. No cloud. No waiting on a SaaS to process your space.

01

On-site · capture

I arrive with iPhone Pro (LiDAR) and Osmo Pocket 3 (gimbal). For each room: a slow LiDAR walk for geometry, a 4K gimbal video walk for texture, a few hand-held detail shots for hotspots. 20-30 minutes per typical room. Anchor markers placed for stitching.

~1-3 hours on site · depending on size
GEAR

iPhone 14 Pro · Polycam LiDAR mode
Osmo Pocket 3 · 4K/30fps gimbal walk
Belkin stand · optional, for sweep shots

02

Process · geometry

LiDAR frames stitched into a clean per-room mesh. COLMAP runs on the Osmo video to recover camera positions and build a dense point cloud. The mesh is the truth source for dimensions and room layout.

~1-2 hours · Mac mini M4 Pro, local
TOOLS

COLMAP · sparse + dense reconstruction
trimesh · mesh cleanup, GLB export
CloudCompare · multi-room alignment

03

Train · the photoreal

Gaussian splatting on a cloud GPU (Lambda Labs / Modal). The frames + camera poses feed a neural model that learns the actual light, texture, and reflections. What comes out is photoreal — not a stitched panorama, not a flat mesh. The room, as the room is.

~10-15 min on cloud GPU · A100 / H100
CLOUD

nerfstudio + splatfacto
Data goes up, splat comes back
~A$2-3 per scene · 10-15 min runtime

04

Stitch · the rooms

Per-room splats + LiDAR meshes aligned using the anchor markers I placed on-site. Multi-room scene exported as a single navigable world. Hotspots added at key objects (TV, bed, kitchen, etc.) with click-to-reveal info cards.

~1 hour
OUTPUT

flatbread.splat · compressed splat
flatbread.glb · Draco-compressed mesh
flatbread.hotspots.json · 2-4 per room

05

Deliver · your link

Self-hosted microsite. The link works on desktop, mobile, and any WebXR-capable headset. The viewer is custom-built, DadPunk-styled, mobile-first, no app install. You get the URL, the embed code, and the raw assets. Hosting is one-shot, no monthly fee.

48h from on-site · sometimes faster
YOU GET

yourdomain.com/walk/ · live tour
<iframe> · for your listing
raw .splat / .glb · for re-use
