Print Lab

The print pass from the now-playing rig, with the model taken out. Same shader maths — a rotated halftone screen, two ink plates slightly out of register, flat quantised ink — running in WebGL on whatever you drop in.

INK PUNCH0.75
PREP / LANE SIM0.85
SCREEN ANGLE45°
REGISTRATION DRIFT1.00

Punch drives the screen: dot presence, coarseness (3.2 → 4.6 px cell at 512), and how flat the ink goes. It barely touches colour fringing — scaling that with the same knob is what turns a print into digital glitch.

Prep / lane sim is doing the job the diffusion model did in the rig. That shader was tuned on the model's output — 512 px, soft, five or six flat colours, measured at 5.6 local contrast. Feed the same screen a sharp photograph (40.9) and it has to fight detail instead of building tone, so it reads as a filter. Prep flattens the input the same way: downsample, blur, reduce to a palette. It is not a model and does not claim to be.

Angle is the screen angle. 45° is classic black; two plates at 30° to each other is what makes a rosette.

Drag and drop works anywhere on the frame.
